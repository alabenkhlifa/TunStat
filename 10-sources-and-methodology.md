# Sources, definitions and methodology

## Cut-off and approach

Research was completed through **29 July 2026**. The dossier prioritizes:

1. Tunisian statistical, ministerial, parliamentary, legal and utility sources;
2. primary multilateral and development-finance sources;
3. official national news agency reporting for recent events or official interviews; and
4. reputable secondary reporting only where primary detail was unavailable.

Figures are not treated as current simply because a page remains online. Each chapter identifies the period represented. Forecasts, program targets, declared investment, approved financing and realized outcomes are separated.

## Health-reference policy

Every tracked or explicitly missing metric has a health definition in [the metric health reference](data/metric_health_reference.csv). Each definition identifies whether it is:

- a legal or prudential minimum;
- an official Tunisian or financed-program target;
- an international reference that is not automatically binding on Tunisia;
- a TunStat country-specific policy or monitoring range; or
- a directional or contextual test where no defensible fixed threshold exists.

The `healthy`, `watch`, `stress`, `gap` and `context` assessments are analytical summaries, not automatic policy triggers. See [Healthy ranges and benchmark methodology](12-health-benchmarks.md) for the benchmark hierarchy, core sources and limitations.

## Definitions

### Actual

A value reported for a completed period or executed transaction. It can still be provisional and revised.

### Forecast

A projection based on assumptions. World Bank 2026–2028 macro values are forecasts, not realized data or Tunisian government targets.

### Declared investment

A proposed or registered project and expected jobs. It does not prove that financing, construction or employment occurred.

### FDI or international investment

A recorded cross-border investment concept. Definitions may include equity, reinvested earnings or intercompany flows depending on the reporting framework. It is not interchangeable with declared projects.

### Budget expenditure

Government expense classified under the budget. The 2025 chart excludes lending operations. The total state budget can include principal repayment and Treasury operations, so headline totals differ.

### Public debt

The Ministry’s provisional stock at end-2025. Debt-to-GDP can differ slightly between the Ministry and World Bank because of timing, GDP estimates and coverage.

### Subsidy

The Ministry’s execution category. The gap between an enterprise’s average sale price and accounting cost is not automatically equal to budget subsidy.

### Energy independence

Primary energy resources divided by primary energy demand. The Energy Ministry reports ratios including and excluding the transit-gas royalty. The chart uses the reported headline including the royalty.

### Electricity production mix

Domestic generation through May 2026. Imported electricity is reported separately as a share of local-market availability and is not included as domestic production.

### Project status

Status is based on the latest reviewed evidence. “Financed” does not mean “under construction”; “strategic” does not mean “approved”; and “under construction” does not mean on schedule.

## Calculations

The dossier calculates only simple, reproducible values:

- H1 non-energy deficit = total deficit − energy deficit;
- investment “other” = declared total − reported industry − services − renewables;
- chart shares and year-on-year differences from published values; and
- principal/interest shares of debt service.

Calculated values are labeled in the CSV files and chart notes.

## Limitations

- The cut-off is a point in time; July electricity operations and project status can change quickly.
- Some official data are provisional.
- No complete public outage microdata were found.
- Public-enterprise consolidated audited accounts and cross-arrears were not available in one reviewed source.
- Project cost figures may use different scope, currency dates and financing packages.
- Legal databases can lag amendments; consolidated texts should be verified before drafting.
- Recommended targets are policy proposals unless explicitly identified as official program targets.
- The dossier does not model household-level distribution because no microdata exercise was performed.
- The coverage audit classifies a metric as current only when a recent public observation was found. “Partial” includes stale, incomplete, non-recurring or non-comparable coverage. “Missing” means no current public value was found in the reviewed sources; it does not prove that the institution lacks internal data.
- The dossier does not provide a project-by-project engineering audit or debt-sustainability model.

## Reproducibility note

The website uses Chart.js 4.5.1 for responsive, interactive charts. Each chart also exposes an exact-value HTML table. The SVG charts remain available for the research documents and non-JavaScript use. CSV files are the source tables. If a newer official release becomes available, update the CSV and health assessment first, then the interactive chart, SVG fallback and narrative, while preserving the actual/forecast/status labels.
